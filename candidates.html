為什麼不直接上圖Why candidates stay off the map

候選資料保留線索，但正式地圖只收錄可回查、可定位、可判斷狀態的案件。Candidate files preserve leads; the active map requires traceable, locatable, status-verifiable records.

候選檔曾明定：若主管機關來源、工程範圍、狀態、期程、位置、去重鍵或 geometry 尚未完整，就維持 candidate。這一頁把這些缺項公開列出，方便之後逐案補資料。Repository review rules keep a record as a candidate when agency sources, scope, status, dates, location, dedupe keys, or geometry are incomplete. This page exposes those gaps for follow-up verification.
